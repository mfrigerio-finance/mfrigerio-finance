## Evidence classification

### Public implementation
Source code, tests or repository material can be reviewed directly.

### Sanitised case study
Architecture and decisions are described publicly while client-specific or sensitive material is removed.

### Professional capability
Supported by CV / portfolio material, but should not be interpreted as a claim of production ownership for every named platform or regulatory framework.

## Important distinction

A platform appearing in the professional landscape does **not** automatically mean sole end-to-end production ownership. The profile distinguishes broad professional capability from public implementation evidence and avoids converting knowledge, participation or cross-functional exposure into unsupported ownership claims.
