# Professional Portfolio

**Finance · Capital · Transformation · Systems · Engineering**

This is a public, sanitised view of capabilities represented across my finance, transformation and engineering work.

Most client work has been delivered under confidentiality agreements. This repository therefore does **not** publish client names, private infrastructure, credentials, proprietary source code or confidential operating data. It focuses on methods, systems thinking, decision frameworks and selected implementation evidence that can be discussed publicly.

## 1. Executive Finance & Capital

Strategic finance, CFO-office support, FP&A, accounting, group reporting, treasury, capital allocation, valuation, risk and executive decision support.

Typical scope includes budgets, rolling forecasts, scenarios, variance analysis, close, reconciliations, consolidation, intercompany, liquidity, working capital, DCF, NPV, IRR, WACC and decision-ready management reporting.

## 2. Finance Transformation & Enterprise Systems

Finance operating models, process redesign, SAP/ERP/EPM environments, requirements, integration, UAT, controls, adoption and cross-functional delivery.

Platform landscape includes SAP S/4HANA FI/CO, SAP Group Reporting, Oracle Fusion, NetSuite, Dynamics 365, Workday Financials, Oracle EPM / Hyperion, SAP BPC, SAP Analytics Cloud, Anaplan and OneStream.

## 3. Data, BI & Automation

SQL, data models, ETL/ELT, Power BI, Python, quantitative methods, workflow automation, reconciliation and AI-assisted finance workflows.

The emphasis is on preserving financial meaning through source ownership, data quality, lineage, validation, reconciliations and explicit exception handling.

## 4. Software & Systems Engineering

Python backends, FastAPI, PostgreSQL, typed REST APIs, WebSockets, modular architecture, security boundaries, deterministic domain logic and automated verification.

Public evidence includes the [Workflow Engine](https://github.com/mfrigerio-finance/workflow-engine) and the [FastAPI Job Service](./examples/fastapi_job_service).

## 5. Security & Control by Design

WebAuthn/FIDO2 concepts, hashed sessions, scoped authorization, company/entity isolation, immutable audit evidence, validation and fail-closed behaviour.

## Core proposition

The common thread is the ability to connect **financial meaning, operational reality, data and software execution**.

Typical work starts with a business or financial question, makes assumptions and ownership explicit, translates them into controlled processes or systems, and leaves behind outputs that can be reconciled, tested and operated by others.

## Selected evidence

- International mandates since 2019 across finance, transformation, technology and cross-border execution.
- CFO-level decision support, planning, forecasting, liquidity, investment evaluation and executive reporting.
- Finance and reporting workflows connecting accounting, operations, ERP systems and financial/non-financial data.
- **40% stated reduction in manual reporting time** through Excel, Power Query and VBA automation.
- Hands-on engineering across Python, FastAPI, PostgreSQL, typed APIs, security boundaries, data models and automated tests.
