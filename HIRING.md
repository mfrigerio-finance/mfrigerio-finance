# Hiring Brief

This page is for recruiters, hiring managers and founders who want a fast view of fit.

## Profile

**Executive Finance & Technology Leader / Senior Software & Systems Engineer**

Cross-functional profile spanning finance leadership, international accounting, FP&A, treasury, finance transformation, ERP, data, automation and backend systems.

## Best-fit problems

I am strongest where an organisation needs someone who can connect several layers at once:

1. **Understand the business and financial problem.**
2. **Model the economics, controls and decision logic.**
3. **Translate requirements into systems, data flows or operating processes.**
4. **Work directly with finance, engineering, vendors and executives.**
5. **Leave behind something controlled, explainable and repeatable.**

## Practical differentiators

- Finance depth combined with hands-on software and data fluency.
- Comfortable with both executive stakeholders and technical implementation detail.
- Strong focus on explicit assumptions, traceability, controls and failure behaviour.
- Experience working across confidential international mandates and cross-functional environments.
- Able to move between strategic work and operational execution rather than remaining at only one layer.
