This page is designed for recruiters, hiring managers, founders, CFOs and transformation leaders who want a fast view of fit.

## Profile

**Executive Finance, Transformation & Systems Leader**

Cross-functional background spanning strategic finance, international accounting, FP&A, treasury, finance transformation, ERP/EPM, data, automation and systems architecture.

## Problems I am best suited to own

I add the most value where an organisation needs someone who can:

1. **Understand the business and financial objective.**
2. **Translate it into drivers, controls, requirements and acceptance criteria.**
3. **Connect accounting, operational and non-financial data.**
4. **Work across finance, technology, vendors and executive stakeholders.**
5. **Make outputs reconcilable, explainable and operationally usable.**
6. **Move from diagnosis into implementation and continuous improvement.**

Typical examples include planning redesign, management-reporting improvement, ERP/EPM transformation, finance-data integration, reconciliation, close/process improvement, treasury and liquidity analysis, and controlled automation.

## Practical differentiators

- **Finance first, technically fluent.** Technology is used to improve finance delivery rather than treated as an isolated discipline.
- **Executive to implementation depth.** Comfortable moving from decision support and stakeholder discussions into process, data and system detail.
- **Control-aware delivery.** Reconciliation, ownership, approvals, segregation of duties, traceability and exception handling are part of the design.
- **Cross-functional execution.** Able to translate between finance, engineering, ERP specialists, operations, suppliers and management.
- **Evidence discipline.** Assumptions, ownership scope and unsupported claims are kept separate.
